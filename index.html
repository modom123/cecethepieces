The Artist

About Cece

The Concept — From the Stage to the Studio

My work lives where reality meets abstraction. As a former dancer, the fluid motion of resin became my new choreography — one form of expression traded for another. In abstract realism the magic happens in the layers: a recognizable form on the base, veiled by translucent waves and "smoke" of resin, creating a depth a photograph can barely capture. I don't chase perfection; I chase connection. Every pour is intentional, organic, and one of a kind.

I'm Cecilia — "Cece" — and before I ever picked up a resin bottle, I told stories with movement. Born in Chicago and shaped by the community of Renton, Washington, I spent years as a professional background dancer and choreographer.

When the world slowed down during the pandemic, I turned to art as a form of healing. What began as a personal sanctuary quickly became a profound passion — and today Cece's Pieces is more than a small business. It's a community-driven studio working in abstract realism and mixed media.

Whether I'm crafting an original gallery work or delicately preserving ashes and flowers into a memorial piece, my mission stays the same: to turn memories into tangible art. Every piece is completely original — when you own a Piece, you're holding a fragment of a story, a heartbeat, and hope, with a certificate of authenticity to prove it.

How I Work

The Process

01
🧪
Materials First
Epoxy resin, alcohol inks, pigment powders, gold leaf, glass — combinations that react in beautiful, unpredictable ways.
02
⏳
The Pour
Resin is poured, tilted, pushed. Heat tools coax movement. Every pour is a negotiation between intention and accident.
03
✨
The Cure
48–72 hours of curing time. No shortcuts. The chemistry needs time to do what chemistry does.
04
✦
The Seal
Sanded, sealed, sanded again. Glass-smooth and UV-resistant. Certificate of authenticity with every work.
A Special Category

Memorial Pieces

I create handcrafted memorial pieces because I believe the way we honor our loved ones should be as unique as they were. No exact duplicates, but a piece can be recreated in a similar design for multiples.

Custom colors, personal touches, embedded botanicals and metals can be worked in. The result is something that truly belongs to a specific person and a specific story.
